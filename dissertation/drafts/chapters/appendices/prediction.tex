\chapter{Prediction}
\label{app:prediction}

The tables below report the predicted scoreline for each of the 31 knockout-stage fixtures of World Cup 2026 under both methods described in Section~\ref{sec:performance-comparison}, together with the actual result. The group-stage fixtures are omitted here for space and are recorded in the repository at the commit-specific path listed in Appendix~\ref{app:code}. For each match, the table reports the modal scoreline under each method, that is, the scoreline $(h, a)$ with the highest probability in the one-step-ahead score grid; this can disagree with the modal three-way outcome (home win, draw, away win), which is the quantity scored by the Brier score and outcome accuracy in Section~\ref{sec:performance-comparison}.
