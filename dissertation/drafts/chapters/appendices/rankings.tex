\chapter{Rankings}
\label{app:rankings}

This appendix collects the top-twenty pre- and post-tournament team rankings produced by the EKF and RB-SQMC methods described in Section~\ref{sec:performance-comparison}, together with the FIFA Men's World Ranking \citep{fifaworldranking2026} used as an external descriptive reference in Section~\ref{sec:performance-comparison}. The model rankings are obtained by ordering the teams by their filtered total strength, the sum of the attacking and defensive components, at the relevant time point; a larger defence coordinate represents stronger defence, so the sum ranks attacking and defensive quality jointly. The top twenty are shown for compactness rather than the full rankings of all 48 teams; the complete rankings are recorded in the repository at the commit-specific path listed in Appendix~\ref{app:code}.
