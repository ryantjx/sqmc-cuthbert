SQMC requires deterministic function of the previous state and a fixed-dimensional vector of uniforms.

$$X_t = \Gamma_t (X_{t-1}, U_t) \qquad U_t \sim \mathcal{U}[0,1]^d$$

$U_t$ generated by low-discrepancy sequences such as Sobol or Halton. 

Hilbert sort is performed during the resampling step. hilbert sort particles and then use QMC points to resample particles. This preserves low-dicrepancy sequence.
